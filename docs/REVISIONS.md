## What the numbers show

Every difference between this project's figures and NHS England's published monthly commentaries sits in one of the three months above.

| Month | Measure | Published commentary | Supplied file | Difference |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Oct 2025 | Total attendances, booked and unbooked, all types | 2,397,701 | 2,401,266 | +3,565 |
| Oct 2025 | Attendances within 4 hours, all types | 1,777,827 | 1,781,392 | +3,565 |
| Oct 2025 | All-department % within 4 hours | 74.1 | 74.19 | +0.09 |
| Dec 2025 | Total attendances | 2,327,015 | 2,326,139 | -876 |
| Dec 2025 | Over-12-hour decision-to-admit waits | 50,775 | 50,789 | +14 |
| Mar 2026 | Total attendances | 2,432,253 | 2,432,272 | +19 |
| Mar 2026 | Booked attendances | 80,936 | 80,925 | -11 |

The months with differences are the months reported as revised, which is consistent with the commentaries having been written before the revisions. That is a coincidence of months and not proof. The original and revised files have not been compared, so it cannot be shown that the revisions account for these exact differences. The signs also differ, since October is higher in the file and December is lower.

The differences are at most 0.2% of attendances and do not change any conclusion. They are disclosed and not adjusted. All other months checked, which are the Type 1 percentages for 8 months, match exactly.

Figures from this project may therefore differ slightly from the original commentaries for those months. The raw files are unmodified.
